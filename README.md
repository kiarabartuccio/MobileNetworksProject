## Topic
Mobile Networks: LTE, 5G, Signal Strength, and Dead Zones

## Project Description
Our project focuses on understanding how mobile networks work, from the radios, towers, frequencies, and the carrier network behind them. It focuses on how technology operates. Dealing with cells, sectors, base stations, carrier network, dead zones, antennas, LTE, and 5G technology.

We will use both of our iPhones to measure the cellular signal strength in two separate locations and discover one dead zone where the reception is weak or unavailable.

We will use Field Test Mode to get the signal measurements and compare our results with mobile carrier coverage maps to understand why signal strength changes depending on a location.
